Privacy Policy

Belle Vista Insurance Agency

Last Updated: December 2025

1. Introduction

Belle Vista Insurance Agency ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and interact with our services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our website or services.

Your continued use of our website following the posting of revised Privacy Policy means that you accept and agree to the changes.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Complete contact forms or applications
  • Request a quote or insurance coverage information
  • Subscribe to our newsletter or marketing communications
  • Make inquiries about our services
  • Interact with customer service
  • Create an account on our website
  • Participate in surveys or promotions

This information may include:

  • Full name and contact information (email, phone number, mailing address)
  • Date of birth and Social Security Number (for insurance underwriting)
  • Insurance history and claims information
  • Payment information and billing addresses
  • Driver's license number and driving history (for auto insurance)
  • Property details (for homeowners insurance)
  • Health-related information (for health insurance)
  • Employment information
  • Any other information you choose to provide

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information through various technologies:

  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: We use cookies, web beacons, pixels, and similar tracking technologies to remember your preferences, understand how you use our website, and deliver targeted content.
  • Log Data: Our servers automatically record information such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on pages, referring URL, and device information.
  • Usage Analytics: We collect data about your interactions with our website, including search queries and click patterns.
  • Location Data: With your permission, we may collect general location information from your device.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from third-party sources, including: Insurance rating agencies and databases; Credit reporting agencies; Underwriting partners and carriers; Data brokers and public records; Marketing partners; Social media platforms (if you link your account); Your insurance carriers and other service providers.

2.4 Children's Information

Our website is not directed to individuals under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will promptly delete such information and take steps to cease collection from that individual.

3. How We Use Your Information

Belle Vista Insurance Agency uses the information we collect for various purposes, including:

  • Providing Services: Processing insurance quotes, applications, and policy administration
  • Customer Communication: Responding to inquiries, confirming transactions, and providing customer support
  • Marketing and Advertising: Sending promotional materials, newsletters, and product updates (only with your consent where required)
  • Insurance Underwriting: Assessing risk and determining eligibility and rates for insurance coverage
  • Claims Processing: Handling insurance claims and dispute resolution
  • Legal Compliance: Fulfilling legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations
  • Fraud Prevention: Detecting and preventing fraudulent activity and unauthorized access
  • Website Improvement: Analyzing website usage patterns to enhance user experience and functionality
  • Research and Analytics: Conducting internal research and analysis to improve our products and services
  • Personalization: Customizing content and recommendations based on your preferences and behavior
  • Data Security: Protecting our systems, networks, and data from unauthorized access
  • Legal Defense: Establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Compliance)

For users subject to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we process your personal information based on: Contract Performance; Legal Obligation; Legitimate Interests; Consent; Vital Interests; and Public Task.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. Retention periods include:

  • Insurance Policy Information: Retained for the duration of the policy and typically 7 years after policy termination.
  • Claims Information: Retained for 7 years from the date of claim closure.
  • Financial Records: Retained for 7 years for tax and audit purposes.
  • Website Cookies: Retained per cookie settings (typically 1-2 years).
  • Marketing Communications: Retained cho đến khi you unsubscribe.
  • Customer Service Records: Retained for 3 years.
  • Deleted Data: May be retained in backup systems for 60-90 days.
